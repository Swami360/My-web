Business Ecosystem & Systems Theory Project

Context & Challenge: Modern enterprises operate in highly disrupted environments characterized by rapid digital transformation, strict privacy regulations, and shifting stakeholder expectations. Objectives & Goals: To evaluate business professionalism through a systems theory lens, examining corporate reputation, intellectual property protection, and stakeholder relationship management.
